Redesign and development of the artists’ website: g-n.fi



When I started this project in summer 2023, the old website’s content had not been updated for three years, and its design has remained the same since its creation 20 years ago. The main goal was to make the website responsive for mobile devices and to improve usability while maintaining its minimalistic visual appearance.





Redesign & Development


The accordion design offered a space-saving solution to list the artists’ career spanning over 30 years with more than 120 documented exhibitions and public commissions. Furthermore, I created a responsive design for different screen sizes. While the mobile view has only one column where pictures are arranged below the texts, on the tablet and desktop view text and images are separated into two columns. In addition, font sizes change dynamically which improves legibility especially on wider screens. For the front page, I created a slideshow of outstanding images and videos of the artists’ latest works.

Other tasks included formatting images into web-ready files, editing the texts provided by the client, editing and embedding vimeo videos, and designing a logo for the website's favicon.
